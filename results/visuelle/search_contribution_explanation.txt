2. Contribution of the search signal
------------------------------------
Each model is compared with its search twin on identical rows. The 95% CI comes
from a weekly bootstrap (2000 draws). MAE_change = with search - without search,
so a negative value means search helped.

model    period      h   MAE change   95% CI              significant
ridge    validation  4   -0.0007      [-0.0026, -0.0000]  yes (tiny gain)
ridge    validation  8   +0.0055      [+0.0029, +0.0070]  yes (worse)
ridge    test        4   -0.0010      [-0.0038, +0.0002]  no
ridge    test        8   +0.0018      [-0.0004, +0.0030]  no
xgboost  validation  4   +0.0038      [+0.0001, +0.0089]  yes (worse)
xgboost  validation  8   +0.0006      [-0.0062, +0.0070]  no
xgboost  test        4   -0.0020      [-0.0097, +0.0038]  no
xgboost  test        8   +0.0054      [-0.0060, +0.0149]  no

Conclusion: the search signal gives no reliable improvement. On test every CI
contains zero. The changes are within about +/-2% of MAE and their sign flips
between horizons and models. At 8 weeks search is slightly worse for both models.
Search is better on about 49-51% of rows, which is a coin flip. The same-week
lag test (lags 0-8) agrees: the best lag is 6 weeks and gives -0.19 pp WAPE,
CI [-0.69, +0.36].

Why search adds little here
- Coverage: the VISUELLE series end 2019-12-16, so only about 69% of test weeks
  have a search value (own keywords: about 67%).
- Aggregation: items are category, colour and fabric totals. Search tags are
  product-level, so the match is loose.
- The sales history (last values, seasonal calendar features) already explains
  most of the variance, which leaves little for search to add.
- Extra search features (lags h..h+4) add noise for 75 items and few weeks.

3. How to read the figures
--------------------------
01: bars per model; the xgboost bars are lowest and the search bars match their
    twins.
02: example items in the test season. xgboost tracks the actual series; the
    search and no-search lines are almost on top of each other.
03: points are MAE changes with CI bars. Bars crossing zero mean no clear effect.
04: items where search helped or hurt most. The effect is spread both ways,
    which suggests noise rather than a systematic signal.

Caveats
- The stock mask is a proxy: stock reconstructed from restocks, with some sales
  recorded in weeks marked out of stock.
- The season split is by label; calendar release dates overlap.
- XGBoost is used (LightGBM is not).
- "No evidence of gain" is not proof of no effect: this is a coarse aggregate
  test with partial search coverage.
